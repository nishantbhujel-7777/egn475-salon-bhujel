Bella Vista Salon Appointment Booking Prototype
Project Option
Option B: Bella Vista Salon — Coral Gables
Problem Statement
Bella Vista Salon currently handles appointments through phone calls, messages, and a manual appointment book. This can cause double bookings, missed details, and extra work for reception staff, while clients may have to wait for a response to find an available time. Our prototype will let clients view salon services and request an appointment for a selected date and time, while staff can review appointments and confirm, complete, or cancel them, so that clients can book more conveniently and staff can keep the schedule organized.
Target Users / Personas
Sofia Martinez — Client
- Goals: Find a service, see appointment options, and book a convenient time online.
- Frustrations: Calling or messaging to check availability and waiting for a response.
- Technology comfort: High.
- Quote: “I want to choose a service and book a time that works for me without having to call back and forth.”
Daniel Rivera — Receptionist
- Goals: Keep the appointment schedule organized and avoid overlapping bookings.
- Frustrations: Interruptions from calls and messages, and the risk of losing track of changes in a manual schedule.
- Technology comfort: Moderate.
- Quote: “I need one clear schedule so I can manage bookings without losing track of changes.”
Must-Have Features
1. View salon services, descriptions, and durations.
2. Select a service to book.
3. Choose a requested appointment date and time.
4. Enter basic client contact details.
5. View appointment details and status.
6. Staff can view the appointment calendar or list.
7. Staff can confirm, complete, or cancel appointments.
8. Staff can add or edit salon services.
Not in This Prototype
- Online payments, deposits, or refunds.
- SMS or automated email reminders.
- A native iOS or Android application.
- Loyalty points, coupons, or referral rewards.
- External calendar integration.
- Payroll or inventory management.
Scope
This is a prototype intended to demonstrate the core appointment-booking and staff scheduling workflow. Features outside the lists above are not included in the initial version.
